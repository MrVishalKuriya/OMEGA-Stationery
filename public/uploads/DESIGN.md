## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet luxury, physical delight, and mindful ritual of artisanal stationery into a tactile digital environment. Rooted in deliberate craft, paper grain, and archival ink, the design balances the classic authority of heritage publishing with the warmth and approachable clarity of modern lifestyle design.

### Aesthetic Persona & Influence
- **Tactile Warm Minimalism:** Generous breathing room, authentic paper-like tonal tiers, and gentle contrast that avoids sterile, clinical tech tropes.
- **Crafted Elegance:** Inspired by custom bookbinding, fine-nib pens, hot-stamped debossing, and creamy cotton paper stocks.
- **Friendly Sophistication:** Elevated editorial typography paired with rounded, friendly UI shapes and soft interactions to foster confidence and calm contemplation.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for creators, writers, designers, and discerning stationery lovers who view notebooks and desk tools as an extension of their creative mindset. The UI induces a sense of unhurried focus, tactile warmth, precision, and enduring quality.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic stationery metaphor: rich archival carbon ink set against soft, unbleached cotton rag paper, structured by whisper-quiet border strokes and energized by traditional vermilion wax seal accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#111113` - Charcoal Ink):** Deep, warm onyx ink used for dominant text hierarchies, primary actionable elements, and sharp graphic anchors. Avoids harsh digital pitch black.
- **Neutral (`#F9F8F6` - Soft Paper Base):** The primary canvas hue, providing an authentic warmth reminiscent of premium notebook parchment and linen covers.
- **Secondary (`#8C7355` - Raw Leather & Ochre):** A grounded natural tone representing bookcloth, leather bindings, and warm metallic accents like brass rules.
- **Tertiary (`#DC2626` - Wax Seal Vermilion):** A crisp, confident red accent reserved for essential focal cues, editorial badges, cart triggers, and live status points.
- **Supporting Layers:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as a lifted surface tier for cards, menus, and elevated trays, separated from the canvas via `#E7E5E0` subtle paper rules.

## Typography

The typographic strategy marries classical prestige with clean contemporary legibility.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Display & Headings (Bodoni Moda):** Directly echoes the iconic high-contrast serif structure of the brand mark. The extreme contrast between thin and thick stems, refined brackets, and sharp serifs evokes fine typesetting, traditional press sheets, and bespoke monogramming.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced humanistic geometry, generous counters, and crisp optical legibility on screens. It ensures dense catalog listings, product descriptions, cart matrices, and forms remain inviting and effortlessly scannable.
- **Micro-Labels & Meta Attributes:** Uppercase, tracked small labels (`letter-spacing: 0.06em`) add an archival catalog feel akin to embossed serial numbers and product specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts in this design system mimic the generous margins of luxury art books and curated architectural catalogs. Negative space is treated as an active material that frames products with quiet authority.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Max canvas container capped at `1360px` centered. 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and a minimum canvas margin of `3rem` (`48px`). Wide outer margins preserve the feeling of book margins.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standard 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margin. Multi-column cards collapse gracefully to dual-column grids.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Hero narratives favor stacked compositions with full-width tactile imagery and generous vertical gaps.

### Spacing Scale & Rhythm
- Spacing relies on a foundational 4px/8px rhythm. Vertical component spacing employs asymmetrical roominess: titles receive generous clearance above (`space-xl`), while supporting metadata stays tightly bound below (`space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic blur blurs or floating tech glass, depth is expressed through **tactile paper layering and warm ambient diffusion**.

### Layering Philosophy
1. **Canvas Tier (Base):** The foundational substrate is `#F9F8F6` with an optional micro-grain tactile texture.
2. **Elevated Sheets (Surface):** Cards, modal drawers, and dropdown menus utilize `#FFFFFF` (Pure Paper) resting on the base canvas.
3. **Tactile Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted and deeply diffused to simulate paper sheets resting on a smooth wooden desk:
   - *Resting Card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 17, 19, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 17, 19, 0.02)`
   - *Lifted Hover / Floating Popover:* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(35, 27, 18, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(35, 27, 18, 0.03)`
   - *Modal & Sheet Depth:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 17, 19, 0.14)`
4. **Hairline Precision:** Low-contrast borders (`1px solid #E7E5E0`) ensure crisp tactile bounds even in subtle lighting environments, preventing edge washouts between cream and white surfaces.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, tailored architectural contouring** (`roundedness: 1`). Elements reject sharp, aggressive corners as well as childish bubble pills, opting instead for gentle corner softening reminiscent of trimmed notebook corners, curved leather folios, and smoothed wooden pen rests.

- **Standard Buttons, Inputs, & Tags:** `0.25rem` (`4px`) to `0.375rem` (`6px`) soft radius.
- **Product Cards & Floating Surfaces:** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) provides balanced framing for photography without clipping inner assets.
- **Focal Overlays & Dialogue Trays:** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`) for generous tactile containment.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to round counter badges, swatch color pickers, and circular icon triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#111113`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`, padding `12px 24px`. On hover, shifts with ease to subtle warm charcoal `#262524` with a gentle 1px upward lift.
- **Secondary (Paper Outline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #111113`, text `#111113`. Hover fills with `#111113` and flips text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#111113`, underlined with a delicate `1px` offset border that animates outward from center on hover.
- **Accent Action (Wax Stamp):** Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`, reserved exclusively for urgent conversions (e.g., checkout completion or exclusive batch drops).

### Cards
- **Product & Story Cards:** Base background `#FFFFFF`, enclosed in a hairline `1px solid #E7E5E0` border. Images use warm, desaturated lighting against cream backgrounds. On hover, the container undergoes an imperceptible transition: shadow gently deepens, and product imagery scales up by `1.015x` over `350ms` using cubic-bezier `(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F9F8F6`, border `1px solid #D5D2CC`, text `#111113`, placeholder `#9C9890`. Padding `12px 16px`. Active focus transitions to a crisp `#111113` border accompanied by an unblurred `0 0 0 1px #111113` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal square (`3px` radius) or circle controls in `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #6E6B66`. Checked state fills `#111113` with a crisp `#FFFFFF` tick mark.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Material Tags:** Background `#F2EFEB`, text `#6E6B66`, label typography (`11px` bold uppercase, `0.04em` tracking). Selected filter chips invert to `#111113` background with white text.
- **Exclusive / Limited Batch Chip:** Background `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08)`, text `#DC2626`, border `1px solid rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Cart Rows
- Separated by horizontal `1px solid #E7E5E0` hairlines. Metadata elements feature strict baseline alignment, pairing title text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 15px with monetary figures formatted cleanly without heavy visual noise.

### Micro-Interactions & Motion
- All state changes use organic easing (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`) across `180ms` to `250ms`, mimicking the natural damping of mechanical desk objects and paper sliding into an envelope.
